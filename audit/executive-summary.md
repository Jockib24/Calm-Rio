## Market Position

### Royan (Listing 1)
- **Current positioning:** Generic "new beach apartment" — no reviews, minimal trust signals.
- **Ideal positioning:** Family/couple beachfront apartment 100m from Grande Conche with dedicated workspace.
- **Gap:** Title lacks specificity (no "Grande Conche", "terrasse", "famille", "télétravail"). Zero reviews = no social proof. Photos unknown in full count.

### Saint-Trojan-les-Bains (Listings 2 & 3)
- **Current positioning:** Family villas for 6 guests in a quiet, green setting near the beach.
- **Ideal positioning:** Premium/new-build family villas between forest and ocean, pet-friendly, remote-work ready, with garden and EV charging (listing 2).
- **Gap:** Listing 3 title is extremely generic ("Maison individuelle 6 pers") and lacks all differentiators. Listing 2 is strong but could add "Oléron", "jardin clos", "télétravail" to the title.
