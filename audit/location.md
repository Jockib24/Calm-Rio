## Location SEO Recommendations

1. **Embed exact distances** in listing descriptions (beach, market, bakery).
2. **Name specific beaches** (Grande Conche, Gatseau, Maumusson).
3. **Mention local events** (Festival à Royan, Fête du Mimosa).
4. **Create neighborhood guide** on direct website with maps.
5. **Add Schema.org GeoCoordinates** for each property.
6. **Add Google Maps embed** on direct booking site.
