# Visual Audit

## Methodology
- Photos were extracted from the Airbnb listing DOM where available.
- Full gallery access requires opening the photo viewer; only viewport-loaded images were captured.
- Scoring is based on composition, lighting, emotional appeal, and conversion likelihood.
