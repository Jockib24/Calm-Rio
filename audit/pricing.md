| Returning guest | 5–10% off direct bookings |
| Shoulder season bundle | 3-night weekend packages |

---

## Revenue Management Rules

1. **Minimum stay:** 2 nights in low season; 7 nights Saturday-to-Saturday in July-August.
2. **Gap nights:** Reduce minimum stay for single open nights.
3. **Event pricing:** Increase rates during Festival à Royan, Fête du Mimosa, 15 August fireworks.
4. **Dynamic pricing:** Use a tool (e.g., Beyond Pricing, PriceLabs, Wheelhouse) or manual weekly review.
5. **Channel parity:** Keep Airbnb/Booking.com/direct rates consistent after commission.

---

## Fee Transparency

- Cleaning fee should be clearly stated and aligned with local market (€60–€120 for 3BR).
- Pet fee: optional but recommended (€25–€50/stay or included if pets allowed).
- EV charging: keep metered supplement model for listing 2; market as eco-friendly feature.
