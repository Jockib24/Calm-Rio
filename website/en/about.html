Our Story

From Royan to Oléron — places we love and want to share

CR

How It All Started

CalmRio started from a simple observation: on Airbnb, we found soulless rentals. Elsewhere, luxury without warmth. We wanted something in between.

Mélanie arrived in Royan on a March morning a few years ago. She was looking for an apartment for herself — she found a raw 60m² space, no charm in sight, with a terrace overlooking nothing. But if you looked closely, you could see the sea between two buildings. She signed that same day. Six months of renovations later, the apartment became what it is: a place where light pours in from everywhere, where you hear seagulls from bed, where people come for a weekend and leave having already booked for the next year.

Agnès, on the other side of the bridge in Saint-Trojan, is in her fifth season. She wanted her villa to feel like a family home — not a showroom. A garden where kids can run barefoot, a terrace where you have breakfast overlooking the pines, and enough bikes in the garage for everyone to explore the island without renting. She started with one house, then a second — because guests kept coming back and there was no room left.

Today, the two of us run everything, up close. We greet guests ourselves, answer messages, change a lightbulb at 10pm if needed. Because that's the idea: rentals where we do things properly, but stay down to earth.

Satisfaction

We chose Royan and Saint-Trojan because we're attached to them. Not for marketing, not as a concept. Mélanie settled here years ago and never left. Agnès has known Oléron since childhood — she came on vacation with her parents, and now she welcomes other families.

Every property has its story. The Royan apartment was a tired rental we rebuilt room by room, spending hours choosing the right tiles, the right countertop, the right bedding. The Saint-Trojan villa is a 1970s house we transformed without stripping its soul — we kept the beams, the terracotta tiles, the spirit.

People often ask us what our secret is. There isn't one. We clean ourselves, we're available, and we don't make promises we can't keep. If we say there's wifi, there's wifi. If we say the beach is 100m away, you can time it.

We don't sell perfect vacations. We sell places we'd want to be ourselves — and we maintain them as if we had to live in them.
Our Hosts

Passionate hosts at your service

We live nearby, we know every corner, and we're here when you need us.

Mélanie

Host — Royan

Mélanie is the one who found the Royan apartment when no one else wanted it. She renovated it corner by corner, getting involved in everything — from choosing the bathroom tiles to setting up the wifi router. She lives 5 minutes away, so when you arrive, she's the one greeting you, showing you how the coffee machine works, pointing you to the best route to the beach. She replies to messages evenings, weekends, even in August. Her extra touch: a binder in the apartment with her personal addresses — the fishmonger she goes to, the restaurant where she takes her friends, the secret spot to watch the sunset.

Agnès

Superhost — Saint-Trojan-les-Bains

Agnès is a force of nature. She runs her two Saint-Trojan houses with a mix of professionalism and warmth that disarms everyone. 4.95★ on Airbnb, Guest Favorite — all that. But what matters to her is that the kids are happy, that parents can finally relax, and that people leave saying "we're coming back next year." She knows the tide schedules, the oyster spots, which markets to visit. She makes the beds, puts fresh flowers, and if you forget something, she drops it off before you even have time to ask.

Our Values

What makes CalmRio different

Nothing theoretical. Three principles we apply every day.

Quality first

We don't rent a room — you get the whole apartment or house, and we want it to be better than home. Professional bedding, a proper kitchen, good wifi. The essentials, done right.

Close to nature

The Royan apartment is 100m from Grande Conche — the city's best beach. The Saint-Trojan houses are a 10-minute bike ride from Gatseau beach, through the forest. We chose these locations because we know them and we know they're the best.

Personalized welcome

We usually respond within 2 hours — even on Sundays. We have baby gear (cot, high chair, gate) if you're traveling with a little one. And pets are welcome in all our properties. Just two rules: let us know, and respect the place.
