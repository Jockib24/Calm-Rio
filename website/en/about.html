Meet Your Hosts

CalmRio was born from a passion for the Atlantic coast and a desire to share its most beautiful secrets.

After years exploring the wild beaches of Île d'Oléron and the colorful streets of Royan, we fell in love with this region we now call home. Every CalmRio property has been chosen for its soul, its exceptional location, and its potential to create unforgettable memories.

We live close to our properties and are always available to welcome you, advise you, and introduce you to the spots only locals know. For us, hospitality isn't a job — it's a philosophy.

Satisfaction

CalmRio was born from a passion for the French Atlantic coast and a desire to share its most beautiful retreats. Every property in our collection has been selected for its unique character, exceptional location, and impeccable comfort.

From the contemporary apartment facing Grande Conche in Royan to the family villas between sea and forest in Saint-Trojan-les-Bains, we have designed these places as havens of serenity for families, couples, and remote workers seeking authenticity.

We believe the best holiday memories are born in places where you instantly feel at home.
