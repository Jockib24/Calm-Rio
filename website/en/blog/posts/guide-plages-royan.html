Guide to the Most Beautiful Beaches of Royan

Our selection of the 5 must-see beaches of the jewel of the Atlantic

BeachesJune 15, 20265 min read

Guide to the Most Beautiful Beaches of Royan

Royan, jewel of the Atlantic coast, is renowned for its fine sandy beaches stretching for miles. Whether you're a swimmer, surfer, or simply looking for a little slice of paradise to relax with family, the Royan coast has something for everyone. Here's our guide to the 5 most beautiful beaches in Royan, tried and tested by the CalmRio team.

1. Grande Conche — The Iconic Beach

You can't talk about Royan without mentioning Grande Conche. With its 2.5 kilometers of fine sand, it's the most famous beach in the city. It stretches from the harbor to Pontaillac, offering stunning views of the Gironde estuary and the Cordouan lighthouse in the distance.

Lined with the casino and numerous restaurants, Grande Conche is ideal for family days. You'll find beach clubs for children, beach volleyball courts, and a lively pedestrian promenade perfect for evening strolls. Swimming is supervised in high season, and the water is generally calm thanks to the bay's conch-shaped configuration.

Good to know: free parking nearby, disabled access along the entire promenade. Arrive before 10 AM in July-August to get the best spots.

2. Plage du Chay — The Family Spot

Nestled at the foot of limestone cliffs, Chay Beach is a true little gem. More intimate than Grande Conche, it's particularly popular with families for its calm, shallow waters. The cliffs provide natural wind protection, making it an ideal spot even when the sea breeze picks up.

Another major plus: the natural saltwater pool that forms in the rocks at low tide. Children love splashing around safely while parents enjoy the setting. The beach also has a small snack bar and restrooms.

Ideal with children: calm, shallow water protected from the wind. Bring beach shoes to explore the rocks.

3. Plage de Pontaillac — Surfer's Vibe

Pontaillac is Royan's trendy neighborhood, and its beach is no exception. With consistent waves, it's the surf spot of the Royan coast. Several surf schools offer lessons for all levels, from beginners to advanced.

The atmosphere here is livelier than elsewhere: beach bars, feet-in-the-sand restaurants, and a jetty extending into the ocean for fishing enthusiasts. In the evening, Pontaillac beach transforms into a gathering spot to admire spectacular sunsets over the Atlantic.

4. Plage de Nauzan — Wild Side

Located between Royan and Saint-Palais-sur-Mer, Nauzan Beach is a haven of preserved nature. Less crowded than its neighbors, it offers a wilder setting with its wooded cliffs and crystal-clear waters. The beach is nestled in a natural cove, giving it a special charm.

It's the ideal beach for those seeking tranquility, away from the tourist hustle. The coastal path along the cliffs offers beautiful walks with breathtaking panoramic views of the ocean. Shaded parking is available in the immediate vicinity.

CalmRio tip: bring a mask and snorkel — the rocky seabeds are teeming with marine life. The beach is also very enjoyable in September, when temperatures are still mild and the crowds have gone.

5. Plage de Saint-Georges-de-Didonne — The Most Family-Friendly

Just south of Royan, Saint-Georges-de-Didonne beach is an immense crescent of fine sand stretching over 3 kilometers. It's undoubtedly the most family-friendly beach in the region: vast, well-equipped, and bordered by a pine forest offering natural shade areas welcome during hot summer days.

The lively seafront offers ice cream parlors, crêperies, and restaurants for all budgets. Children will enjoy the mini-golf, playground, and the little tourist train that runs along the coast. Swimming is supervised and access is facilitated by numerous parking lots.

Ready to Explore Royan's Beaches?

Royan is an exceptional seaside destination that deserves much more than just a quick visit. Each beach has its own personality, atmosphere, and strengths. Whether you're a couple, family, or group of friends, you'll surely find your slice of paradise along this magnificent coast.
