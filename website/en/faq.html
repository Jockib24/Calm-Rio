Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

General

Yes, pets are welcome in all our properties. Please let us know in advance so we can prepare the space and inform you of any specific conditions.

Yes, free on-site parking is available at every property. No additional charge.

Check-in is from 4:00 PM. For the Saint-Trojan villa, check-in is possible between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM. Check-out is before 10:00 AM for all properties.

Yes, all our properties have high-speed WiFi. The Saint-Trojan villa also offers a dedicated workspace with desk, monitor, and HDMI cable — ideal for remote work.

Yes, bed sheets and bath towels are provided in all our rentals and included in the booking price.

Yes, baby equipment (cot, high chair) is available on request. Please let us know before your arrival so we can prepare everything.

Royan — Apartment

The apartment is approximately 100 meters from Grande Conche beach, the most beautiful beach in Royan with 2.6 km of fine sand.

Yes, the building has an elevator. The apartment is easily accessible.

Yes, the main bedroom features a desk with monitor and HDMI cable, ideal for remote work with a sea view.

No, the apartment does not have air conditioning. However, its design and natural ventilation provide pleasant comfort, and the proximity of the ocean brings a refreshing breeze.

Saint-Trojan — Villa

The villa is located between the forest and the ocean. The village, oyster port, and beaches are accessible by bike in 5 to 10 minutes via cycle paths.

Yes, the villa features a fully enclosed private garden, a 50m² south-facing wooden terrace, and a 17m² covered terrace — perfect for outdoor dining and children.

Yes, an EV charging point is available. A supplement based on the meter reading is requested at the end of your stay.

No, but the villa is designed with excellent natural ventilation. Fans are available for your comfort.

The villa can accommodate up to 6 guests. For larger groups, it is possible to also book the identical house located opposite — subject to availability. Contact us for more information.

Saint-Trojan — House

The house is located near the cycle path that leads to the beach and the village center. The village is just a few minutes away.

Yes, the house has a wooded garden and a terrace, perfect for enjoying the mild climate of Île d'Oléron.

Yes, depending on availability, it is possible to book both houses together for large families or groups of friends. Contact us to check availability.

Booking

You can book directly on this website or via Airbnb. Direct bookings often offer the best rates, with no additional platform fees.

Cancellation terms depend on the stay dates and booking platform. Please check the booking page for the exact terms applicable to your stay.
