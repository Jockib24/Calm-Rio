Between sea and forest

Imagine cycling through the Saint-Trojan state forest, the scent of maritime pines mingling with Atlantic sea spray. Île d’Oléron is a paradise for cyclists, with over 130 km of bike paths winding between salt marshes, oyster farms, and wild beaches.

In Royan, Grande Conche unfurls its 2.6 km of fine sand just 100 meters from our apartment. A morning swim, a coffee on a terrace facing the ocean, a sunset stroll — here, the sea is part of everyday life. The famous Marennes-Oléron oysters and Charentes pineau await you at the covered market.

Local Guide — The Côte de Beauté

From the beaches of Grande Conche to the forests of Île d’Oléron, explore the treasures of the Atlantic coast.

Beaches

Grande Conche, Gatseau beach, Maumusson beach… Swimming, lounging, and unforgettable sunsets on fine sand.

Cycling

Île d’Oléron, a cycling paradise with over 130 km of bike paths through forests, marshes, and oyster-farming villages.

Gastronomy

Marennes-Oléron oysters, fresh fish from the port, Charentes pineau, Charentaise galettes… An exceptional terroir.

Culture & Heritage

Royan central market, Notre-Dame Church, Fort Boyard, Saint-Trojan Little Train, festivals, and maritime heritage.
