Local Guide — Côte de Beauté & Île d'Oléron

Between ocean and forest, discover the treasures of our region

Beaches

Grande Conche Beach

The most beautiful beach in Royan with its 2.6 km of fine sand. Ideal for families, swimming, and sunset strolls.

Royan100m
Beaches

Gatseau Beach

Wild beach at the southern tip of Île d'Oléron. Accessible by the Little Tourist Train or by bike through the state forest. A preserved gem.

Île d'OléronAccess by bike or train
Beaches

Pointe de Maumusson

Where the ocean meets the Pertuis de Maumusson. A striking natural spectacle with stunning views of the coast and the waves. Ideal spot for photographers.

Île d'OléronAccess by bike
Gastronomy

Marennes-Oléron Oysters

Marennes-Oléron oysters are renowned worldwide. Visit the oyster shacks at the port of Saint-Trojan and taste them facing the ocean with a glass of pineau.

Saint-TrojanOn-site tasting
Gastronomy

Royan Covered Market

Iconic shell-shaped building, listed as a historic monument. Fresh produce, fish, seafood, and Charentais specialties. An unmissable experience.

RoyanEvery day
Gastronomy

Saint-Trojan Market

Covered market in the heart of the village. Local products, cheeses, seasonal fruits and vegetables in an authentic Oléron atmosphere.

Saint-Trojan1 min
Nature

Saint-Trojan State Forest

Immense maritime pine forest covering much of the southern part of the island. Paradise for cyclists, hikers, and mushroom pickers in autumn.

Saint-TrojanCycle paths
Nature

Estuary Park (Parc de l'Estuaire)

Nature reserve at the mouth of the Gironde. Migratory bird watching, discovery trails, and exceptional panoramic views over the estuary.

Saint-Georges-de-DidonneBirdwatching
Nature

Île d'Oléron Marshes

Salt marshes and bird sanctuary in the northeast of the island. Peaceful walks among egrets, herons, and avocets in a unique landscape.

Île d'OléronOrnithological sanctuary
Culture

Notre-Dame Church of Royan

Masterpiece of post-war modernist architecture, nicknamed the "concrete cathedral." Its ship's sail silhouette dominates the Royan seafront.

RoyanHistoric monument
Culture

Fort Boyard

The famous maritime fortress, made globally known by the TV show. Visible from the north coast of Île d'Oléron. Boat excursions available.

Pertuis d'AntiocheBoat excursions
Culture

Saint-Trojan Little Train (Le P'tit Train)

Historic tourist train that crosses the Saint-Trojan state forest to Gatseau Beach. A picturesque ride enjoyed by young and old since 1963.

Saint-TrojanFamily activity
Activities

Cycling on Île d'Oléron

Over 100 km of cycle paths through the forest, vineyards, and along the coast. The island is a paradise for cyclists of all levels. Bike rental in the village.

Île d'Oléron100km of paths
Activities

Un Violon sur le Sable Festival

A one-of-a-kind classical music festival: symphony concerts on Grande Conche Beach, facing the ocean. A magnificent event every summer in Royan.

RoyanJuly — August
Activities

Mimosa Festival (Fête du Mimosa)

Iconic winter celebration in Saint-Trojan-les-Bains in February. Flower parades, entertainment, and festive atmosphere when the mimosa lights up the island in yellow.

Saint-TrojanFebruary

Through the seasons

The region in every season

Each season reveals a different facet of the Atlantic coast.

June — August

Summer

The ultimate beach season. Swimming, lounging on fine sand, and spectacular sunsets. July and August are peak season.

September — November

Autumn

Ideal season for forest cycling, oyster tasting, and mushroom picking. Mild temperatures and golden light.

December — February

Winter

Invigorating walks on deserted beaches, Mimosa Festival in February. The region is calm, peaceful, and authentic in the off-season.

March — May

Spring

Wildflowers blanket Île d'Oléron. Perfect season for cycling, birdwatching, and the first swims.
