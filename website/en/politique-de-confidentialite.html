Privacy Policy

How we protect your personal data

1. Data Collection

We collect the personal data you provide when using the contact form or the booking process: first name, last name, email address, phone number, and information relating to your stay.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Processing your booking requests
  • Responding to your questions via the contact form
  • Sending you information relating to your stay
  • Improving our services

3. Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of your data is based on your consent (contact form) and on the performance of a contract (booking).

4. Retention Period

Your data is kept for the duration necessary to manage your request or booking, then archived in accordance with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with the applicable data protection regulations (GDPR / French Data Protection Act), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@calm-rio.com.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of your personal data.

7. Contact

For any questions relating to data protection, you can contact us at contact@calm-rio.com.
