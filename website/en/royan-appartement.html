About this property

New 60m² apartment with a 20m² terrace combining a beachside and contemporary style, very bright, ideally located 100m from Grande Conche beach and close to many downtown shops.

Large living area with lounge and kitchen/dining room. Comfortable sofa, fully equipped kitchen with wine cooler. All windows open onto the terrace — lounge area, dining area, tree-lined — perfect for al fresco dining.

Bedrooms

The apartment has two bedrooms: the first with a large 160cm bed, TV, and workspace equipped with a monitor and HDMI cable; the second with two 90cm single beds, ideal for children or friends.

Bathroom

Modern bathroom with Italian shower. Separate laundry room equipped with washing machine, dryer, iron, and ironing board. Independent WC for added comfort.

Ideal for

This apartment is perfect for couples, families with children, remote workers, and beach lovers. The elevator makes access easy, and the free on-site parking is a real plus in Royan.

Yes, the main bedroom has a dedicated desk, monitor, and HDMI cable for remote work.

Yes, pets are welcome in the apartment. Please notify the host in advance so we can prepare the space.
