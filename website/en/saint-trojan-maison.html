About this house

Detached house for 6 guests comprising a bright living/dining room, three comfortable bedrooms — two with 160x200cm beds and one with two 90x200cm beds — a functional kitchen, a bathroom, a separate WC, a terrace, and a wooded garden.

Ideally located near the bike path that serves the beach and the village center of Saint-Trojan-les-Bains. Île d'Oléron offers kilometers of safe cycle paths, perfect for family outings.

Home Comfort

Bed sheets, bath towels, and tea towels are provided for your comfort. Baby equipment is available on request (cot, high chair). Pets are welcome.

Ideal for families

This house is designed for families. The wooded garden offers a safe space for children. For larger families, the identical house opposite can also be rented, bringing total capacity to 12 guests (subject to availability).

Frequently asked questions

How far is the beach?

The house is located near the cycle path that leads directly to the beach and the village center of Saint-Trojan-les-Bains.

Is there a garden?

Yes, the house has a pleasant wooded garden and a terrace for al fresco dining.

Can both houses be rented together?

Yes, subject to availability, the identical house opposite can be rented simultaneously for large families or groups, bringing total capacity to 12 guests.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome in this house. Please notify the host in advance.

Are linens provided?

Yes, bed sheets, bath towels, and tea towels are provided and included in the rental price.

Is baby equipment available?

Yes, baby equipment (cot, high chair) is available on request. Please let us know before your arrival.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM. Check-out must be before 10:00 AM.

Ready to book your stay?

Contact us or book directly online. The best rates are available with direct booking.
