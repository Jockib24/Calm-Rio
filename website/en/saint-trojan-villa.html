About this villa

Modern house in Oléron style, combining Charentais island and contemporary style, very bright, with beautiful volumes, ideally located between sea and forest. 110m² on a tree-lined enclosed garden, with 50m² south-facing wooden terrace — garden furniture, parasol — and 17m² covered terrace with teak table. Barbecue available.

Spacious living/dining room with high ceiling, bay windows opening onto terraces. Open-plan equipped kitchen: dishwasher, refrigerator, oven, microwave, induction hob. Utility room with washing machine, dryer, and freezer.

Bedrooms

3 bedrooms of 12m² each: two with 160cm beds, one with two 80cm beds. Each bedroom is designed for comfort with soothing natural-toned decor.

Bathroom

Modern bathroom with Italian shower and separate suspended WC. Bed sheets and bath towels provided.

Remote Work

A desk equipped with an HDMI monitor is available for working in an exceptional setting, between forest and ocean.

Electric Vehicle

A charging point for electric or hybrid vehicles is available on-site. A supplement based on the meter reading is requested at the end of your stay.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the beach?

The villa is located between the forest and the sea. Gatseau beach is accessible in 5 to 10 minutes by bike via cycle paths.

Is there a garden?

Yes, the villa features a fully enclosed, tree-lined private garden with a 50m² south-facing wooden terrace (garden furniture, parasol) and a 17m² covered terrace with a teak table.

Is EV charging available?

Yes, a charging point for electric or hybrid vehicles is available on-site. A supplement based on the meter reading is requested at the end of your stay.

Can we book for a large group?

This villa accommodates up to 6 guests. For larger families, the identical house opposite can also be booked (6 additional guests), bringing total capacity to 12 guests, subject to availability.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome. The enclosed garden is perfect for them. Please notify the host in advance.

Are linens provided?

Yes, bed sheets and bath towels are provided and included in the rental price.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM. Check-out must be before 10:00 AM.

Ready to book your stay?

Contact us or book directly online. The best rates are available with direct booking.
