# FAQ — Vacation Rentals in Royan & Saint-Trojan-les-Bains

## General Questions

### Are pets allowed?
Yes, all three properties accept pets. Please let us know in advance so we can prepare the space.

### Is parking included?
Yes, free parking on premises is available at every property.

### What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 16:00 (16:00–20:00 for the Saint-Trojan villa). Check-out is before 10:00.

### Is WiFi provided?
Yes, all properties include WiFi. The Saint-Trojan villa also offers a dedicated workspace with an HDMI screen.

### Are bed linens and towels included?
Yes, bed linens and towels are provided at all properties.

### Is baby equipment available?
Yes, baby equipment is available on request. Please notify us before your arrival.

## Royan Apartment

### How far is the beach?
The apartment is approximately 100m from the Grande Conche beach.

### Is there an elevator?
Yes, the building has an elevator.

### Is there a dedicated workspace?
Yes, the main bedroom includes a desk, screen, and HDMI cable for remote work.

### Is the apartment air-conditioned?
No, the apartment does not have air conditioning.

## Saint-Trojan Villa

### How far is the beach?
The villa is located between the forest and the sea. The village, port, and beaches are accessible by bike in 5–10 minutes.

### Is there a garden?
Yes, the villa has a private, fully fenced garden plus a 50m² wooden terrace and a 17m² covered terrace.

### Is EV charging available?
Yes, an EV charging point is available. A supplement based on meter reading is requested at the end of the stay.

### Is the villa air-conditioned?
No, but the villa is designed with good ventilation. Fans or reversible AC are recommended additions based on guest feedback.

### Can I rent the villa for a large group?
The villa accommodates up to 6 guests. For larger families, please contact us about the identical house across the street.

## Saint-Trojan House

### How far is the beach?
The house is close to the cycling path, beach, and village center (exact walking distance unknown).

### Is there a garden?
Yes, the house has a garden and terrace.

### Can I rent both Saint-Trojan houses together?
Yes, depending on availability, the identical house across the street can be rented for large families or groups.

## Booking & Cancellation

### How do I book?
You can book directly through this website or via Airbnb. Direct bookings often offer the best rates.

### What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellation terms depend on the dates and platform. Please check the booking page for exact conditions.

### Do you offer long-stay discounts?
Yes, monthly discounts are available for remote workers and extended stays.
